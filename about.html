About Next Step Edu

Hi, I'm Mike

I'm the developer behind Next Step Edu. I've taught English online for over 20 years, and at the start of 2020 I taught myself to code. Since about 2022 I've been publishing apps and games on the Google Play Store.

I started with apps for English learners, built from what I saw in my own lessons: the tools students actually need to practice. Since then I've branched out into games, and this website now hosts two web-based projects.

My Projects

🖼 MVP Connect

A free tool that turns photos into interactive, spoken albums. Add tags and voice recordings to each image so every photo tells its story. It runs in any browser with no account needed, and is useful for families, caregivers, teachers and speech and language support.

Learn more about MVP Connect

🎨 Lounges

A shared online whiteboard where up to three people can draw, play games like Pictionary and create together in real time, from anywhere in the world.

Learn more about Lounges
